Tag Out

Scan the device's Tag Out label (or its printed serial barcode). Staff and admins then scan the borrower's ID card; students who are signed in check out straight to their own account. USB and Bluetooth scanners type straight into this field.

Register a device

Copy the serial exactly from the device sticker or system info. Placeholders, repeats and duplicates are rejected. The app can't confirm the number matches the physical device, so check it by eye.
